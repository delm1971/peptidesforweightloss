Mechanism of Action: Dual-Agonist vs Single-Agonist

The core clinical distinction between Zepbound and Wegovy lies in their pharmacological targets:

  • Wegovy (Semaglutide): A selective GLP-1 (Glucagon-Like Peptide-1) receptor agonist. It mimics natural gut hormones to delay gastric emptying, increase satiety, and suppress appetite centers in the brain.
  • Zepbound (Tirzepatide): A dual GIP (Glucose-Dependent Insulinotropic Polypeptide) and GLP-1 receptor agonist. By activating two distinct metabolic pathways simultaneously, it achieves enhanced metabolic regulation and superior fat breakdown.

Clinical Trial Outcomes: Which Wins for Weight Loss?

In head-to-head clinical trials (SURMOUNT-5), Zepbound demonstrated clear superiority in overall weight reduction compared to Wegovy:

  • Total Weight Loss: Participants on Zepbound achieved an average weight loss of 20.2% (~50.3 lbs) over 72 weeks, compared to 13.7% (~33.1 lbs) for participants on Wegovy.
  • Significant Weight Reduction Targets: Over 64% of patients taking Zepbound achieved at least 15% body weight loss, whereas only 40% reached that milestone on Wegovy.
  • Relative Efficiency: Overall, Zepbound delivered 47% greater relative weight reduction than Wegovy over equivalent timeframes.

Dosing Schedules & Administration

Both medications are administered once weekly via subcutaneous injection, starting at a low introductory dose and gradually titrating upward every 4 weeks to minimize digestive side effects.

Zepbound Dosing Ramp

Starts at 2.5 mg weekly for 4 weeks, titrating in 2.5 mg increments (5 mg, 7.5 mg, 10 mg, 12.5 mg) up to a maximum maintenance dose of 15 mg weekly.

Wegovy Dosing Ramp

Starts at 0.25 mg weekly for 4 weeks, titrating through 0.5 mg, 1.0 mg, and 1.7 mg steps up to the standard 2.4 mg weekly maintenance dose.

Side Effect Profiles & Safety

Because both treatments act on gastrointestinal hormone receptors, their side effect profiles are remarkably similar. Most reported adverse effects are mild to moderate and diminish as your body adjusts during the titration phase.

  • Common Side Effects: Nausea, mild diarrhea, constipation, acid reflux, and temporary stomach discomfort.
  • Discontinuation Rates: Clinical trials showed comparable discontinuation rates between both drugs due to side effects, confirming that Zepbound's higher weight loss does not come at the expense of higher intolerance.
