Top Hair Loss Treatments: How They Work

1. Finasteride — The DHT Blocker

Finasteride works by inhibiting 5-alpha reductase, the enzyme responsible for converting testosterone into DHT. By lowering DHT levels in the scalp, Finasteride protects hair follicles from miniaturization, halting hair loss and allowing weak follicles to recover.

  • Who Qualifies: Men experiencing male pattern baldness along the crown, vertex, or receding hairline.
  • Realistic Expectations: Daily use typically prevents further hair loss within 3 to 6 months, with visible regrowth appearing between 6 to 12 months.

2. Minoxidil — The Growth Re-Energizer

Minoxidil is a vasodilator that widens blood vessels around the hair follicles, increasing the flow of oxygen, blood, and nutrients. This action shortens the resting (telogen) phase of the hair cycle and extends the active growth (anagen) phase.

  • Who Qualifies: Men noticing general thinning or early-stage follicle miniaturization. Available in topical and oral formulations.
  • Realistic Expectations: Reduced shedding and initial fine hair growth typically become visible after 3 to 4 months of consistent daily use.

3. Combination & Emerging Therapies

Combining Finasteride (blocking DHT) with Minoxidil (stimulating circulation) offers a dual-action approach that delivers higher success rates than using either treatment alone. Emerging options like topical compounding formulations and specialized peptide blends further minimize systemic side effects while maximizing scalp absorption.
