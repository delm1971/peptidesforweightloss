Which peptides are FDA-approved for weight loss?

The FDA-approved peptides for weight loss include liraglutide (Saxenda), semaglutide injection (Wegovy), semaglutide pill, and tirzepatide (Zepbound), according to GoodRx. These are prescription medications that require medical oversight and cannot be obtained legally without a healthcare provider's authorization.

Semaglutide and tirzepatide are specifically approved for chronic weight management, meaning they are intended for long-term use under clinical supervision. Rite Aid notes that approved products containing semaglutide or tirzepatide have large controlled weight management trials supporting their use.

Tesamorelin has a narrow FDA approval for reducing excess abdominal fat in adults with HIV and lipodystrophy, according to Rite Aid. This approval does not extend to general weight loss or obesity treatment in the broader population. Medical News Today emphasizes that the best peptides for weight loss are those approved by the FDA, including semaglutide.

How do semaglutide and tirzepatide work for weight loss?

Semaglutide and tirzepatide work by mimicking natural hormones that the gut releases after eating. Medical News Today explains that these medications belong to a class of drugs called glucagon-like peptide 1 (GLP-1) receptor agonists, which copy the action of the natural hormone GLP-1.

Semaglutide acts on a single receptor, the GLP-1 receptor, to regulate appetite and reduce food intake. Defiant Health states that semaglutide's single-receptor action focuses on appetite control and long-term weight management.

Tirzepatide acts on two receptors, GIP and GLP-1, instead of one. Defiant Health notes that this dual action gives tirzepatide a broader mechanism for influencing appetite and metabolic regulation. Rite Aid confirms that both medications focus on appetite and long-term weight management.

How much weight can you lose with semaglutide versus tirzepatide?

In clinical studies, semaglutide helped people lose about 15 percent of their body weight, according to Defiant Health. The dual-acting tirzepatide reached up to approximately 22 percent weight loss in clinical research.

Both medications are supported by large controlled trials, as Rite Aid confirms. These percentages represent averages from study populations and do not guarantee the same outcome for every individual.

Individual results vary based on factors including adherence to the prescribed regimen, diet, physical activity, and underlying metabolic conditions. A healthcare provider can help set realistic expectations based on a person's medical history and treatment goals.

Do peptides like sermorelin and tesamorelin help with belly fat?

Sermorelin does not produce fat loss on its own. Defiant Health describes sermorelin as a growth hormone-releasing peptide that may support sleep, recovery, and lean body composition, but it is not a weight loss drug.

Tesamorelin is FDA-approved specifically for reducing visceral fat, but only in people with HIV-associated lipodystrophy. Rite Aid confirms that tesamorelin has an approved role in reducing excess abdominal fat in adults with HIV and lipodystrophy.

Defiant Health states that peptides like sermorelin and tesamorelin are not weight loss drugs; they may support body composition and recovery. BHR Center adds that sermorelin is a synthetic peptide that naturally boosts the body's production of human growth hormone (HGH), which influences body composition rather than direct fat loss.

What should you know about muscle loss during rapid weight loss?

Rapid weight loss can result in losing muscle, not just fat. Defiant Health explains that losing muscle slows metabolism, which can make long-term weight management more difficult.

This connection helps explain why some peptides like sermorelin are used for recovery and lean body composition rather than direct fat loss. Preserving muscle during weight loss supports metabolic health and may improve overall body composition outcomes.

A clinician-guided approach to weight loss should account for muscle preservation alongside fat reduction. Monitoring body composition, not just scale weight, provides a more complete picture of health during treatment.
