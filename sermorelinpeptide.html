How Sermorelin Stimulates Natural Growth Hormone

Sermorelin is a synthetic bioidentical peptide containing 29 amino acids—representing the active segment of naturally occurring Growth Hormone-Releasing Hormone (GHRH). Unlike direct synthetic HGH injections, which introduce exogenous hormones into your system, Sermorelin functions as a targeted signaling molecule.

When administered, Sermorelin binds to specific receptors on the pituitary gland, instructing it to synthesize and release higher amounts of endogenous growth hormone. Because it works through your body's natural endocrine pathways, it preserves natural feedback loops and pulsatile release patterns, drastically minimizing the risks associated with hormone overuse.

Sermorelin vs. Synthetic HGH: The Core Advantage
