Step 1

Step 2
State-Licensed Clinician Review

Telehealth prescribing is strictly regulated at the state level. The physician, nurse practitioner, or physician assistant reviewing your intake must hold an active license in the exact state where you are physically located during the consultation.

  • Consultation Format: Depending on your state's telehealth laws, your provider will review your chart asynchronously via secure clinical messaging or conduct a synchronous video/audio consultation.

A licensed clinician determines whether tirzepatide is safe for you, selects an appropriate starting dose (typically 2.5 mg weekly for standard protocols, or lower for micro-dosing), and orders baseline lab panels if needed.

Step 3
Understand Brand-Name vs. 2026 Compounding Rules

Once approved, your prescription is fulfilled through one of two legal channels under 2026 FDA regulations:

  • Brand-Name Tirzepatide (Zepbound® / Mounjaro®): FDA-approved commercial pens or single-dose vials. Your telehealth clinician can route your prescription to a local retail pharmacy (if covered by insurance) or to manufacturer direct-to-consumer cash-pay programs such as LillyDirect.
  • Patient-Specific 503A Compounding: Because the FDA declared the national tirzepatide shortage resolved, pharmacies can no longer mass-produce generic-style copies of Zepbound. Under Section 503A, a state-licensed compounding pharmacy may prepare custom tirzepatide only when a prescriber documents a specific medical necessity for that individual patient (such as custom titration dosing or sensitivity to a commercial inactive ingredient).

6 Red Flags of Illegitimate Online Sellers

Exit any website immediately if you notice any of the following warning signs:

  • "No Prescription Needed": Selling tirzepatide without a licensed U.S. clinician evaluating your medical history is illegal and unsafe.
  • "Research Chemical" or "Not for Human Consumption" Disclaimers: Unregulated peptide sites sell unsterile powders intended for lab testing, not human injection.
  • Crypto, Zelle, or CashApp Checkout: Legitimate medical clinics and pharmacies process standard credit/debit cards and HSA/FSA payments.
  • Hidden Pharmacy Identity: Never order from a platform that refuses to disclose the name and state license of the dispensing U.S. pharmacy.
  • Salt Formulations: Avoid sellers offering "tirzepatide sodium" or "tirzepatide acetate," which are unapproved active ingredients.
  • Guaranteed Approval Before Review: Ethical clinicians never guarantee a prescription before assessing contraindications.
