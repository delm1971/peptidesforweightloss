Red Flags: What to Avoid When Buying Tirzepatide Online

While legitimate compounded options offer substantial savings, unregulated sources present serious health risks. Exercise caution and avoid platforms displaying any of the following warning signs:

⚠️ Dangerous Online Red Flags

  • No Prescription or Medical Consultation Required: Any site offering to sell tirzepatide without a mandatory evaluation by a licensed healthcare provider is operating illegally.
  • "For Research Chemical Use Only" Labels: Unregulated sellers often list peptides as "research chemicals" or "not for human consumption" to bypass safety checks. These products lack sterility and purity testing.
  • Unusually Low Prices (<$100/month): Legitimate pharmaceutical-grade active ingredients, sterile vial preparation, and provider oversight carry baseline operational costs. Unreasonably cheap prices often indicate counterfeit or diluted product.
  • Lack of U.S. Pharmacy Sourcing: Avoid vendors that do not explicitly state fulfillment by a licensed 503A or 503B U.S. compounding pharmacy.
