1. What Is BPC-157? (Body Protection Compound)

BPC-157 is a synthetic peptide derived from a naturally occurring protein found in human gastric juice. It plays a significant role in protecting the stomach lining and promoting systemic tissue healing.

💡 Key Biological Mechanisms of BPC-157

  • Angiogenesis Acceleration: Promotes the formation of new blood vessels, delivering oxygen, nutrients, and growth factors to damaged tissues with poor blood supply (such as tendons and ligaments).
  • Fibroblast Activation: Stimulates collagen synthesis and cellular migration to repair soft tissue tears and micro-injuries.
  • Gut Health & Anti-Inflammatory Action: Helps restore mucosal integrity in the gastrointestinal tract while dampening local inflammatory signals.

2. What Is TB-500? (Thymosin Beta-4 Synthetic Segment)

TB-500 is a synthetic peptide fragment corresponding to the active domain of Thymosin Beta-4, a naturally occurring peptide present in high concentrations in blood platelets and wound tissue.

While BPC-157 focuses heavily on localized collagen deposition and localized vessel growth, TB-500 acts globally throughout the body by regulating cell structure and mobility:

  • Actin Upregulation: Regulates actin, a key cellular protein essential for cell structure, movement, and repair.
  • Systemic Cell Migration: Enhances the migration of repair cells to areas of acute or chronic damage.
  • Flexibility & Adhesion Prevention: Reduces scar tissue formation, helping maintain tissue elasticity during ligament and joint repair.
