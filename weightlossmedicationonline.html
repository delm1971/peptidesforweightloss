Are You a Candidate? (Eligibility Criteria)

Online prescription platforms adhere to strict clinical guidelines to ensure patient safety before any medication is prescribed. General eligibility criteria typically include:

  • Body Mass Index (BMI) ≥ 30: Classifying as chronic weight management indication.
  • BMI ≥ 27 with Comorbidities: Such as high blood pressure, high cholesterol, or type 2 diabetes.
  • Medical History Review: Absence of absolute contraindications, such as a personal/family history of medullary thyroid carcinoma or MEN 2.

Costs, Insurance Coverage, and Access in 2026

Total monthly investment for online weight loss medications varies based on insurance coverage and prescription path:

  • Self-Pay / Out-of-Pocket: Brand-name options typically cost $900 to $1,300 monthly without insurance coverage.
  • HSA/FSA Eligibility: Most online platform membership fees, medical visits, and medication costs can be paid using pre-tax HSA or FSA funds.
