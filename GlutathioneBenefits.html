Key Health Benefits of Glutathione

1. Master Cellular Detoxification

Glutathione binds directly to heavy metals, pollutants, and metabolic waste products in the liver, converting fat-soluble toxins into water-soluble compounds that can be safely excreted from the body.

2. Immune System Reinforcement

Lymphocytes and white blood cells rely heavily on adequate glutathione concentrations to proliferate and mount an effective defense against pathogens. Maintaining high levels ensures peak immune responsiveness.

3. Cellular Energy & Mitochondrial Support

Mitochondria—the powerhouses of your cells—generate energy while simultaneously producing reactive oxygen species (ROS). Glutathione scavenges these free radicals inside the mitochondria, maintaining optimal energy production and reducing chronic fatigue.

4. Anti-Aging & Skin Radiance

By neutralizing oxidative stress that accelerates skin aging, glutathione helps protect collagen fibers. Additionally, it inhibits the enzyme tyrosinase, promoting an even skin tone, reducing hyperpigmentation, and enhancing natural skin clarity.
