#3 Ranked: CJC-1295 & Ipamorelin Stack

RANK #3 — BODY RECOMPOSITION

Growth Hormone Secretagogues for Lean Body Mass

Clinical Efficacy: Rather than causing dramatic absolute scale weight loss, this combination focuses on body recomposition—increasing growth hormone (GH) output to decrease body fat percentage while preserving or building lean muscle mass.

Mechanism: CJC-1295 acts as a Growth Hormone-Releasing Hormone (GHRH) analogue, while Ipamorelin acts as a Growth Hormone Secretagogue (GHRP). Together, they stimulate the pituitary gland to release natural pulsatile growth hormone without elevating cortisol or prolactin levels.

Verdict: Ideal for athletic individuals or post-GLP-1 patients aiming to trim visceral fat, recover faster, and maintain muscle definition.

#4 Ranked: AOD-9604 (Lipolytic hGH Fragment)

RANK #4 — TARGETED LIPOLYSIS

The C-Terminal Fragment of Human Growth Hormone

Clinical Efficacy: Developed specifically to isolate the fat-reducing region of the human growth hormone molecule (amino acids 177-191). While animal models showed significant lipolytic activity, human clinical trial outcomes have shown mixed overall results.

Mechanism: Stimulates lipolysis (the breakdown of fat) and inhibits lipogenesis (the formation of new fat storage) without causing insulin resistance or altering blood glucose levels.

Verdict: Best used as an adjunctive peptide for targeted stubborn fat loss rather than a standalone solution for significant weight reduction.
