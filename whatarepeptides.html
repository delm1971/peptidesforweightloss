What Are Peptides and How Does Sermorelin Work?

Peptides are short chains of amino acids that serve as biological signaling molecules in the human body[cite: 1]. They instruct specific cells and tissues to perform vital functions—ranging from tissue repair and immune regulation to metabolic signaling[cite: 1].

Sermorelin is a bioidentical peptide analogue of Growth Hormone-Releasing Hormone (GHRH). When administered, it binds to GHRH receptors on the pituitary gland, signaling it to produce and secrete your own natural Human Growth Hormone (hGH).

Determining the Right Sermorelin Dosage

There is no one-size-fits-all dosage for peptide therapy. Sermorelin protocols are customized based on key individual factors:

  • Baseline Hormone Levels & Age: Younger adults may require lower maintenance dosing compared to older adults with significant GHRH decline.

  • Dosing Schedule: Sermorelin is typically administered subcutaneous before bedtime to align with the body's natural nocturnal GH surge.
